# Vendor Escalation Guide

## Support tiers
- **Tier 1 (vendor help desk)**: licence questions, how-to, minor defects.
  Response target: 1 business day.
- **Tier 2 (vendor technical support)**: outages and defects with a
  workaround. Response target: 4 business hours.
- **Tier 3 (vendor engineering)**: data loss, security issues, or an outage
  with no workaround. Response target: 1 hour, 24x7.

## How to escalate
1. Always open the ticket at the tier below before escalating; keep the
   original ticket number — escalation moves the ticket, it does not create
   a new one.
2. For Tier 3, call the vendor's critical line after filing; an unfiled phone
   escalation is not tracked and will be closed.
3. Include: business impact, start time, affected systems, and what you have
   already ruled out.

## Commercial issues
Billing and licence-true-up questions go to procurement, not vendor support.
